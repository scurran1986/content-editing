VOICECAM BACKLOG — skeptical cut (2026-09-08)
Source: PLAN.md "Feature ideas" + creator needs. Points: 1/2/3/5/8 (8 = split).

PREMISE CHECK (read before the stories)
- Voice record control exists natively: Samsung camera ("record video"), iOS
  Voice Control, Apple Watch / $10 Bluetooth clicker, TikTok 3s/10s timer.
  Our only non-native angle: pause/resume/retake across the room mid-talk —
  unproven on the target phone. Everything below serves proving that.
- Browser vehicle: acceptable ONLY because there is no Mac. Costs: Safari mp4
  vs Chrome webm, no lock-screen use, Android Chrome can't run
  SpeechRecognition + getUserMedia together (SO 25920854). PWA = test rig,
  not product. Decide after N1.
- Voice vs tap: tap wins phone-in-hand. Voice wins only hands-free at
  distance. Anything usable within arm's reach: kill.

=== NOW (next 2 sprints, <=3 pts, max 8) ===

N1. iPhone Safari go/no-go                                      [3]
As a creator I want the POC to record + obey voice on my iPhone so the
product isn't a Windows demo.
AC: (1) Safari iOS: "record"/"stop" produce a playable clip. (2) Latency
word-end→state change measured, logged, <1s. (3) Written verdict: ship PWA,
vosk-browser fallback, or stop.
Why exist: Nothing else matters if this fails. Cannot be killed.

N2. Echo self-trigger test + fix                                [2]
As a creator I want playback of my clip not to say "stop" and stop itself.
AC: (1) Clip containing spoken "stop, play, rewind" plays through on phone
speaker without firing. (2) Same clip fires 0 commands at 1.5s debounce OR
recognizer paused during playback. (3) Result in dev.log.
Why exist: PLAN lists it as a hard gate; unverified on device. Native apps
don't have this problem, we do.

N3. False-positive guard while talking to camera                 [2]
As a creator I want to say "...then just stop" mid-take without ending it.
AC: (1) 20-line script with embedded command words, ≤1 false fire.
(2) parseCommand tail rule documented in test_commands.js with 5 new cases.
(3) Optional prefix ("camera stop") toggle costs one line.
Why exist: TikTok has no voice at all, so no precedent — but a recorder that
cuts mid-sentence is worse than a timer. Keep.

N4. "Again" = retake (discard + re-record)                       [2]
As a creator I want to say "again" to bin the last take and start a new one.
AC: (1) "again" while recording/stopped → previous blob dropped, new
recording starts. (2) Status shows take count. (3) test case in test_commands.
Why exist: THE hands-free use case TikTok lacks (TikTok retake = walk to
phone, tap trash, confirm). Smallest real differentiator. Keep.

N5. Keep multiple takes, save chosen one                          [3]
As a creator I want takes 1..n kept so I don't lose a good one to "again".
AC: (1) Takes listed with duration. (2) "play" on a selected take.
(3) Save button downloads the selected take. No editing.
Why exist: iOS Camera keeps every clip in Photos already. Only justified
because "again" (N4) discards — ceiling: last 5 takes in memory.

N6. Countdown on "record"                                        [1]
As a creator I want 3s before recording so I'm in position.
AC: (1) Big 3-2-1 overlay. (2) Skippable via "go". (3) One toggle, on by default.
Why exist: TikTok has a timer natively — but not paired with voice start
across the room. 1 point, tolerated. Kill if N1 fails.

N7. Save works on iPhone (Photos, not Downloads)                 [2]
As a creator I want the clip in my camera roll so I can post it in TikTok.
AC: (1) Safari: navigator.share({files}) lands mp4 in Photos. (2) Chrome
download still works. (3) File opens in TikTok upload.
Why exist: Browser download ≠ camera roll on iOS; clip is trapped otherwise.

N8. Add-to-Home-Screen + wake lock                               [1]
As a creator I want the screen not to sleep mid-take.
AC: (1) wakeLock held while recording. (2) manifest icon/name. (3) Launches
fullscreen from home screen.
Why exist: Native gets this free; browser doesn't. One-liner.
